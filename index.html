How day and night are split. A streak is the time between two interactions, on any device, and is never cut at a clock boundary. A streak counts as night if it overlaps 00:00–05:00 by 30+ minutes. The night then stretches back over any activity from 22:30 (target bedtime) leading into that streak and forward over any activity until 06:45 (target wake time), so a 3am wake-up stays inside the night. Everything else is day: a screen-free 4–10pm stays a day streak even if you go to bed at 10:30. Streaks under 15 minutes are ignored in the tables above. A night is recognised once you’ve been screen-free past about 00:30.

“Combined” only counts a gap when every device was quiet, from when the last device began logging. Per-device figures are measured within the same day or night period; * = that device started logging partway through it. Monthly averages leave out the streak or night still in progress.
